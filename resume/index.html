RESUME GUIDE

What should a professional resume include?

A strong resume should make it easy for a recruiter to understand who you are, what you can do and what experience you bring to the role.

01 Contact information — Name, professional email, phone and relevant links.
02 Professional summary — A concise introduction focused on your strengths.
03 Work experience — Relevant roles, responsibilities and measurable achievements.
04 Education and skills — Qualifications and skills that support the position.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is ResumeCraft?

ResumeCraft is an online resume-building tool that helps you organize your professional information using ready-made resume templates.

Which resume template should I choose?

Choose a layout that is easy to read and suits your target role. A simple ATS-oriented layout can be useful when you want a straightforward structure.

What information should I prepare before creating a resume?

Prepare your contact details, professional summary, employment history, education, skills and relevant links.

Can I customize my resume?

Yes. ResumeCraft's builder is designed to let you choose templates and customize supported design settings while entering your resume information.
