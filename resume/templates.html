RESUME TEMPLATE GUIDE

How to choose the right resume template

Choosing the right resume template depends on your experience, industry and the type of position you are applying for. A good resume template should make your information easy to read and help employers quickly understand your experience and skills.

Professional Resume Templates

Professional templates are suitable for many corporate, technical, finance, business and administrative roles. They provide a structured layout while keeping your experience easy to scan.

Creative Resume Templates

Creative templates can be useful for design, marketing and other visually oriented careers where presentation can complement your professional experience.

ATS-Friendly Resume Templates

ATS-oriented templates use a simpler structure, clear headings and straightforward formatting. This can make resume information easier for applicant tracking systems and recruiters to process.

Minimal Resume Templates

Minimal templates focus on clarity and whitespace. They are useful when you want your experience, education and skills to remain the primary focus of your resume.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Resume template questions

Which resume template should I choose?

Choose a template that is easy to read and matches your target industry. For most professional roles, a clean and structured template is a good starting point.

What is an ATS-friendly resume template?

An ATS-friendly template generally uses a simple structure, standard headings and readable formatting so that resume information can be processed more easily.

Can I customize the template?

Yes. After selecting a template, you can enter your personal information, experience, education and skills in the ResumeCraft builder and customize the supported design settings.

Can I change my template later?

Yes, provided your ResumeCraft builder supports changing the selected template. Your resume information can then be displayed using the new layout.
