Privacy Policy

This page explains how Image Compressor Pro may handle information when you visit our website or use our online tools.

Last Updated: August 2026

At Image Compressor Pro, we aim to provide useful online tools while being transparent about how information may be handled. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to visitors and users of our website.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website and which features or third-party services are enabled, certain technical or usage information may be collected automatically.

This may include information such as browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, approximate usage information and technical information needed to operate, secure and improve the website.

2. Images and Files Uploaded to Our Tools

Image Compressor Pro provides browser-based image processing tools. Where the applicable tool processes an image directly in your browser, the image may be processed locally on your device rather than being uploaded to our servers.

The exact processing method can depend on the tool, browser, file type and technical implementation available at the time you use the service.

Important: Before processing important or sensitive files, please review the tool's behavior and keep your own backup copy when appropriate.

Information that is collected or provided may be used for purposes such as operating and maintaining the website, improving our tools, understanding website usage, preventing abuse, detecting technical problems and responding to user inquiries.

We aim to use information only for legitimate purposes related to providing, maintaining and improving our website and services.

Image Compressor Pro may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember certain preferences, understand website usage and support advertising where applicable.

Some cookies may be placed or managed by third-party services used on the website. Your browser may provide controls that allow you to manage or block cookies.

We may use analytics or similar technologies to understand how visitors interact with the website. This can help us identify technical issues, understand which features are useful and improve the overall user experience.

Analytics providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Advertising providers may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, personalize, measure or limit advertisements, subject to their applicable policies and settings.

The availability and use of advertising services may change over time.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for functions such as hosting, analytics, security, advertising, website performance or other technical purposes.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms and applicable settings. We recommend reviewing the policies of third-party services when their services are used.

Our Resume Builder allows users to enter information such as names, contact details, education, employment history, skills and other resume-related content.

Users should avoid entering information that they do not need for creating their resume and should review their resume carefully before downloading, sharing or submitting it.
