Privacy Policy

Your privacy is simple here: We do not collect, store, or track your data.

Last Updated: August 2026

At Image Compressor Pro, we respect your privacy. This page clearly explains how our website operates and how we handle user data.

1. No Data or File Storage

All tools on Image Compressor Pro (including the Image Compressor and Resume Builder) run 100% locally in your web browser.

We do not store, save, or upload any of your images, files, or resume details to our servers. Everything you process or enter stays completely on your own device and is cleared when you close or refresh your browser.

2. Personal Information

We do not require account registration, login, or personal details to use any of our tools. We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers unless you choose to contact us directly via email.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Image Compressor Pro does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools to monitor or profile users.

We may use basic browser features (such as local storage) solely to save site preferences, such as your selected dark/light color theme.

4. Third-Party Links & Hosting

Our website is hosted on standard web infrastructure to serve static web pages. We do not share any user data with third-party advertising or analytics networks. If our website contains links to external websites, we recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we update or add new features in the future, any changes to our privacy practices will be updated directly on this page with a revised date.

6. Contact Us
