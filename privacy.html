We respect your privacy and aim to keep our data practices simple and transparent.

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Image Compressor Pro handles information when you visit our website or use our online tools, including the Image Compressor and Resume Builder.

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.

Our website may also receive basic technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address and general website usage information.

2. Images and Files

Our Image Compressor is designed to process images in your web browser. When processing is performed locally in your browser, your images are not sent to our servers for processing.

We do not intentionally collect or store the images you process through the browser-based image compression tool.

Please Note: The exact behavior of a tool can depend on its technical implementation and your browser.

The Resume Builder allows you to enter information such as your name, contact details, education, employment history, skills and other resume information.

Resume information may be stored locally in your browser to allow the Resume Builder to remember your work and settings.

Information stored only in your browser is not intentionally collected or stored by us on a remote server.
