Privacy Policy

Learn how Image Compressor Pro handles your information, images, resume data, cookies, and third-party services.

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Image Compressor Pro handles information when you visit our website or use our online tools, including the Image Compressor and Resume Builder.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, you may provide information such as your name, email address, and the details of your message. We use this information to respond to you and provide support.

Our website may also collect basic information automatically when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and device information. This helps us keep the website secure and running properly.

2. Images and Files

Our Image Compressor processes your images directly in your web browser. Your images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers for compression.

We do not collect, view, or store the images you process with our Image Compressor. Your files remain on your device while you use the tool.

Please Note: Your images are processed directly on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

3. Resume Builder

Our Resume Builder allows you to enter information such as your name, contact details, education, work experience, skills and other information needed to create your resume.

Your resume information is saved locally in your browser so that you can continue working on your resume and keep your settings when you return to the website.

Your resume information is stored on your device and is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

4. Cookies and Browser Storage

Our website uses browser storage to remember certain preferences and information entered into our tools. For example, the Resume Builder uses Local Storage to save your resume information and settings on your device.

Our website may use cookies in the future for analytics, advertising or other website functionality. Third-party services may also use cookies when those services are enabled on our website.

You can manage or delete cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. Disabling these technologies may affect some features of the website.

5. Advertising

We plan to use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdSense, to help support and maintain our website.

When advertising is enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure advertisements in accordance with their policies and applicable requirements.

We will update this Privacy Policy if our advertising services or data practices change.

6. Analytics

We do not currently use Google Analytics or other third-party analytics services to track how visitors use our website.

If we add an analytics service in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy to explain how that service collects and uses information.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for specific functions such as website hosting, security or advertising.

These services operate independently and may handle information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Your images and resume information are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to third-party services for processing.

8. How We Use Information

We use the information we receive to operate and maintain our website, provide support, improve our tools, keep our services secure and respond to questions or requests.

We do not use your images or resume information for unrelated purposes. Images processed through our Image Compressor and resume information stored in your browser remain on your device and are not collected by us.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to keep our website secure and protect the information that we receive from users.

Images processed through our Image Compressor and resume information stored in your browser remain on your device and are not stored on our servers.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no website or Internet connection can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. External Links

Our website may include links to other websites or online services. These websites are not operated by Image Compressor Pro and may have their own privacy policies and terms.

We are not responsible for the content, security or privacy practices of websites that you access through external links. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or privacy practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page, and we will change the "Last Updated" date to show when the policy was revised.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
