Privacy Policy

Transparency matters to us. Learn how we protect your files, data, and privacy.

Last Updated: August 2026

At Image Compressor Pro, accessible from our site, the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Image Compressor Pro and how we use it.

1. Local Processing & File Privacy

All online tools offered on this site—including the Image Compressor and Resume Builder—process data entirely within your web browser (client-side processing).

We do not upload, save, view, or store your images, files, or resume details on any remote servers. All files processed stay strictly on your device and are permanently cleared when you refresh or close your browser session.

2. Log Files

Image Compressor Pro follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Like any other website, Image Compressor Pro uses "cookies" and browser Local Storage. These technologies are used to store information including visitors' preferences (such as Dark/Light theme mode settings) and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Advertising

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

However, visitors may choose to decline or opt-out of the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Some of advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our advertising partners include:

  • Google AdSense

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Image Compressor Pro, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that Image Compressor Pro has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers, and advertisers have no access to your locally processed files.

6. Third Party Privacy Policies & Opt-Out

Image Compressor Pro's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. To know more detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers, it can be found at the browsers' respective websites. You can also opt out of personalized ads by visiting www.aboutads.info.

7. Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

We would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, or object to processing of personal data. Because we do not store personal data or user files on servers, your personal data is protected by design.

8. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
