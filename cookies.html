Cookies Policy

This page explains how ImageCompressorPro uses cookies and browser storage.

Last Updated: August 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how ImageCompressorPro uses cookies and similar browser technologies when you visit our website or use our online tools.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites can store in your browser. They help websites remember certain information and preferences.

Websites can also use other browser technologies, such as Local Storage, for similar purposes.

2. How We Use Browser Storage

Our website uses browser storage to remember certain preferences and information entered into our tools.

For example, our Resume Builder uses Local Storage to save your resume information and settings on your device. This allows you to continue working on your resume when you return to the website.

This information is stored in your browser and is not stored on our servers.

3. Cookies for Website Functionality

Cookies may be used when they are needed for website functionality, security, preferences, or other features of the website.

Some website features may not work correctly if cookies or browser storage are disabled.

4. Advertising Cookies

We plan to use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdSense, to help support and maintain our website.

When advertising services are enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display, measure, and personalize advertisements according to their policies and applicable requirements.

Advertising and cookie settings may be managed through the options provided by the relevant advertising service or through your browser.

5. Analytics

We do not currently use Google Analytics or other third-party analytics services to track how visitors use our website.

If we add an analytics service in the future, we will update this Cookies Policy to explain how that service uses cookies or similar technologies.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for specific purposes such as advertising, security, hosting, or other website features.

Third-party services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. These services have their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to manage or delete cookies through the browser settings.

You can usually choose to delete existing cookies, block cookies, or receive a warning before cookies are stored.

You can also clear Local Storage through your browser settings or site data controls.

Disabling cookies or browser storage may affect some features of ImageCompressorPro.

8. Image Compressor

Our Image Compressor processes images directly in your web browser. Your images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers for compression.

Cookies and browser storage are separate from the image processing itself and do not upload your images to our servers.

9. Your Privacy

We aim to keep our use of cookies and browser technologies simple and transparent.

For more information about how we handle information, images, and resume data, please read our Privacy Policy .

10. Changes to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy when our website, services, or use of cookies changes. Any updates will be posted on this page, and we will update the "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Cookies Policy, please contact us.
