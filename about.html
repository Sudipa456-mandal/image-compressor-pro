👥 Who We Are

Image Compressor Pro is a free online image optimization tool designed to help users compress JPG, PNG and WEBP images quickly and securely without noticeable quality loss.


🎯 Our Mission

Our goal is to provide a fast, secure and user-friendly image compression service that works directly in your browser. Your images remain private because they are processed locally on your device whenever possible.


✨ What We Offer

  • ✅ JPG Image Compression
  • ✅ PNG Image Compression
  • ✅ WEBP Compression
  • ✅ Image Resize
  • ✅ Max Width & Height Support
  • ✅ Target File Size Compression
  • ✅ Fast Download
  • ✅ Secure Processing

⭐ Why Choose Us?

We believe image compression should be simple, fast and free. Our tool is designed for students, bloggers, designers, photographers and businesses who need optimized images for websites and social media.
