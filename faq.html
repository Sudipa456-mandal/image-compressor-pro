Frequently Asked Questions

Resume Builder
What is the Resume Builder?

Our Resume Builder makes it easy to create a professional resume by adding your personal details, summary, experience, education, and skills.

Is the Resume Builder free?

Yes. You can use the Resume Builder features without paying a fee.

Can I choose a resume template?

Yes. The Resume Builder offers a variety of resume templates, so you can choose a design that best fits your needs and career goals.

Can I edit my resume after creating it?

Yes. You can edit your resume information and customize it before downloading the final version.

Can I download my resume?

Yes. The Resume Builder is designed to allow you to download your completed resume.

Image Compressor
What is an image compressor?

An image compressor reduces an image's file size while keeping the quality as clear as possible.

Which image formats are supported?

The Image Compressor supports common image formats including JPG, PNG and WEBP.

Will image quality be affected?

Compression can affect image quality depending on the compression settings. You can adjust the available quality settings to find the balance you need.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Your images are processed directly in your browser and are not uploaded to our server. Your files stay on your device while you compress them.
