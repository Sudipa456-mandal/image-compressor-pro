Need to compress an image to exactly 50KB? Our free Image Compressor Pro lets you reduce JPG, PNG and WEBP images without installing any software. Everything works directly in your browser.

Why Compress Images to 50KB?

Many online applications require images under 50KB, including:

  • Government application forms
  • Passport and visa photos
  • Job application portals
  • College admission websites
  • Online exam registration
  • ID card uploads

How to Compress an Image to 50KB

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Click Choose Images.
  3. Select your JPG, PNG or WEBP image.
  4. Set Target File Size to 50KB.
  5. Click Compress Image.
  6. Download the optimized image.
